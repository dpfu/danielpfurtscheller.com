## SortBoard

**Alpha prototype.** Set up visual sorting tasks for workshops and studies with participants. Arrange images, short videos or text cards on a board and replay a sorting session for discussion.

[Open SortBoard](https://dpfu.github.io/sortboard/)

## Folder Sort

**Beta prototype.** Compare images in an existing collection and group them into folders and subfolders. Export the original images in the resulting folder structure.

[Open Folder Sort](https://dpfu.github.io/FolderSort/)

## Video Board

**Alpha prototype.** Explore video through synchronised frame, audio and shot timelines. Arrange clips and stills linked to the original video, and add codes and memos for qualitative analysis.
